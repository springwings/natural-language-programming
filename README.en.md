## What This Book Is About

Programming is going through a fundamental change. In the past, you had to study a programming language for years before you could turn an idea into software. Today, you can use **natural language** — the language you already speak and write — to tell AI what you want, and let it build the software for you.

This is **not** a book of prompt tricks, nor a book asking you to memorize syntax. This book teaches you a **method**:

- Define software like a **product manager** — make vague ideas precise
- Break down problems like an **engineer** — split big goals into executable tasks
- Test like a **user** — find problems and describe them clearly
- Direct AI like a **project lead** — make it change the right thing without making a mess

When you finish this book, you may not write traditional code — but you will have a new ability: **turning an idea into software that other people can actually use.**

## Who This Book Is For

- People with zero programming background who have a software idea
- People who have used AI chat but don't know how to make it actually *build* things
- People who had AI build something but don't know how to change it — or change it correctly
- Non-technical people who want a small tool for work or life
- Anyone who wants to understand what "programming" means in the age of AI

## How to Read This Book

1. Read **Part I** first to build the right mindset — don't rush to code.
2. **Parts II–VI** are the main method. Read in order, practice as you go.
3. **Part VII** has five hands-on projects — follow at least the first one fully.
4. **Part VIII** is for leveling up — come back after you've built something.

Every chapter follows the same rhythm: **scenario → concepts → real dialogue examples → illustrations → common pitfalls → summary & exercises → copyable instruction templates**.

## What Makes This Book Different

- **Dialogue-driven**: Lots of real "what the human says / how AI responds / how the human corrects it" examples, not just abstract advice.
- **Focus on *changing* software, not just *creating* it**: the real pain point for beginners is not knowing how to modify what AI built.
- **Five complete projects**: task manager, expense tracker, AI document assistant, CRM, and your own AI app — all end to end.
- **Bilingual**: Each language is a complete, standalone edition, making reading and collaborative translation easy.
